Pickems is college football pick'em with your crew — private leagues where everyone helps build the slate, then picks against the spread.

NOMINATE, THEN PICK
Each week, members nominate games. The group slate comes from those picks — not a one-person commissioner's spreadsheet. Browse the FBS board, filter Top 25 or by conference, and see the line before you nominate.

AGAINST THE SPREAD
Make your picks ATS, lock before kickoff, and score with the rest of the league. Spreads stay visible on nominations, your card, and group results so nobody is guessing the number.

LIVE SATURDAYS
Follow live scores as games go final. Home Screen widgets and Live Activities keep standings and kickoff close without opening the app.

STANDINGS, AWARDS, DYNASTY
Weekly and season leaderboards, career records, and awards like Sharpshooter, Heartbreaker, and Contrarian. Bragging rights with receipts.

YOUR LEAGUE, YOUR RULES
Invite friends with a code, set slate size and deadlines as commissioner, and talk it out in group chat. Report, block, or delete your own messages — keep the rivalry fun.

Download Pickems, start a league, and make every Saturday count.
