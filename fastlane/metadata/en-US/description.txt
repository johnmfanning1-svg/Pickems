CFB Pickems is the college football pick'em app for private leagues. Join with a code, pick against the spread, and follow live scores every Saturday.

COLLEGE FOOTBALL PICK'EM LEAGUES
Start a league in minutes or join with an invite code. Commissioners set the rules — who selects games, how many games are on the slate, deadlines, and tie-breakers. Everyone plays the same week, without a spreadsheet.

SELECTIONS, THEN PICKEMS
Each week starts with Selections: choose the college football games that make your league's slate. Then make your Pickems — pick who covers the spread on every game. Lines stay on the card so nobody is guessing the number.

LIVE SCORES EVERY SATURDAY
Follow Power 4, Top 25, and conference games with live scores and spreads from ESPN. Filter the board, track your league slate, and see results as games go final.

YOUR TEAM, YOUR LEAGUE
Pick your favorite team and CFB Pickems themes the app around your colors. Invite friends, manage members, and keep weekly standings, season records, and titles in one place.
